\chapter*{Software and AI Tools}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Software and AI Tools}

The field reconstruction was carried out with \texttt{NIFTy} \citep{Edenhofer2024,niftycl} and its
\texttt{JAX} backend \citep{JAX}. Data handling, coordinate transformations and the kernel density
estimates rely on \texttt{NumPy} \citep{Harris2020}, \texttt{SciPy} \citep{Virtanen2020},
\texttt{pandas} \citep{McKinney2010} and \texttt{Astropy} \citep{AstropyCollaboration2022}.
The interactive vectorfield viewer is built on \texttt{three.js} \citep{Threejs}.

The AI assistant Claude \citep{Claude} was used as a tool in three areas: as a programming aid for
the analysis code, which started from an existing implementation of the model by \citet{Hutschenreuter2026} and was modified and
in part reimplemented over the course of this thesis, for the development of the interactive
vectorfield viewer, and for language editing of the manuscript, including rephrasing and
proofreading. All results, their interpretation and the final wording were checked by the author, who takes
responsibility for the content of this thesis.
